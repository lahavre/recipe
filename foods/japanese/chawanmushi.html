Chawanmushi

Japanese savory steamed egg custard — a silky, dashi-rich custard hiding chicken, shimeji, and kamaboko, steamed gently in individual cups.

      Notes

      The custard uses a 1 : 2.5 egg-to-dashi ratio by weight (richer than the usual 1 : 3). If your eggs don't weigh exactly 150g, multiply their weight by 2.5 for the dashi and adjust the seasoning to match.

      Never let the water boil while steaming — keep it at 80–90°C. Too much heat makes the custard rough and pitted instead of silky. Skimming off the air bubbles before steaming matters for the same reason.

      Use thin-walled chawanmushi cups or heat-resistant ramekins; thick cups don't conduct heat well and throw off the timing. Light-coloured (usukuchi) soy sauce keeps the custard pale.

      Vegetarian: skip the chicken and kamaboko, use kombu dashi, add more mushrooms or vegetables, and steam for 15 minutes. Chikuwa, narutomaki, or shrimp can stand in for the kamaboko.

      Using a steamer basket instead of a pot? Wrap the lid in a towel so condensation doesn't drip into the custard. Oven alternative: bake the covered cups in a roasting pan with 2–3cm of boiling water at 180°C.

      Keeps in the fridge, in the cups, for up to 2 days. Reheat by steaming for 2–3 minutes — avoid the microwave, which ruins the silky texture.
