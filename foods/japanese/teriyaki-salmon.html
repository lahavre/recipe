Teriyaki Salmon

Japanese home-style teriyaki salmon — crisp-skinned fillets, steamed briefly in sake to stay juicy, then glazed in a simple four-ingredient sauce.

Prep
5 min
Cook
10 min
Serves
2

    Method

      Notes

      Thin, Japanese-style fillets (about 2cm thick) cook faster and stay juicier. Start skin-side down — the skin browns best in a clean pan.

      The light coating of flour helps lock in the juices and gives the sauce something to cling to as it thickens.

      Take the salmon off the heat at 52–54°C in the thickest part — it keeps cooking from residual heat as it rests and while it's glazed.

      For dairy-free, skip the butter or swap it for more neutral oil. For gluten-free, use cornstarch or potato starch instead of flour, and tamari or gluten-free soy sauce.

      Serve with steamed rice and miso soup. Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days or the freezer for up to 2 weeks — great flaked over rice or tucked into a bento.
